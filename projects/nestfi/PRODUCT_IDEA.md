## 1. Problem / Pain Point
Families lack a centralized way to track and manage household finances. They need visibility into income, expenses, savings, and investments, plus the ability to categorize transactions for analysis.

## 2. Target Users
- Couples and families who share finances
- Households wanting unified financial visibility
- Users managing joint bank accounts and family investments
- Multi-generational families needing shared financial oversight

## 3. Product Vision
NestFi is a web-based family financial management platform that enables households to collaboratively track, categorize, and analyze their finances in real-time.

## 4. Core Use Cases
1. Family tracks household income and expenses with custom categories
2. Family members log transactions (purchases, income, cash withdrawals)
3. Family views dashboard with spending trends, savings rate, investment performance
4. Superadmin creates families and invites initial owner via email
5. Owner accepts invitation and adds other family members
6. User belongs to multiple families and switches between them on login
7. Family manages bank accounts, income categories, expense categories, investment categories

## 5. Must-have Features
- Income and expense tracking with categorization (categories generate statistics)
- Cash withdrawal treated as expense (category: "Cash Withdrawal")
- Dashboard with statistics and analysis
- Superadmin account (default: superadmin/admin123, password changeable)
- Superadmin can create families and invite owners via email
- Email-based invitation and acceptance workflow for owners
- Owners can add members after accepting invitation
- Multi-family support (one user account → multiple families)
- Family switching on login
- Financial management sections: bank accounts, income categories, expense categories, investment categories

## 6. Nice-to-have Features
- Budget tracking and alerts
- Investment portfolio tracking
- Financial goal setting
- Automated categorization suggestions
- Mobile app
- Bill splitting among family members
- Financial reports and exports

## 7. Constraints
- Budget: Not specified (assume startup/self-funded)
- Timeline: Not specified
- Tech preference: Not specified yet (to be confirmed with SA)
- Compliance/security: Email verification required, secure password handling
- Deployment: Web-based (desktop-first, responsive design)

## 8. Success Metrics
- Family members can log in and view household financial overview within 2 minutes
- All family members see consistent transaction data
- Categories enable meaningful spending analysis
- User retention week 1 and month 1
